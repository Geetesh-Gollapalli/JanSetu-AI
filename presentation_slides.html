The Challenge: The Indian Infrastructure Paradox

Disconnected Citizen Telemetry vs. Misaligned Public Capital

Problem Statement

1. Fragmented Grievance Silos

Citizen development requests are scattered across disconnected systems: 2G voice helplines, local Gram Sabha minutes, WhatsApp groups, and CPGRAMS portals.

  • Zero unified national aggregation
  • Dialect and language barriers shut out non-English/Hindi speakers
  • Unstructured voice audio discarded after call logging

2. Blind Public Spending

Central & State capital expenditure (Capex) allocation is frequently conducted in administrative silos, detached from hyper-local ground reality.

  • Funds allocated to low-urgency projects
  • Acute crises (arsenic water, submerged bridges) remain unaddressed for years
  • ₹12,480 Cr capex deficit across 16 frontier aspirational districts

3. No DPI Impact Measurement

Governments lack a closed-loop mechanism to measure whether completed capital assets genuinely solved the citizen distress that provoked the petition.

  • No citizen corroborate loop
  • Inability to simulate budget rebalancing impacts in real time
  • Violations of modern data privacy (unredacted Aadhaar/phone storage)
Key Insight for National Policymakers: India does not have an absence of citizen voices. India has an absence of an intelligent, privacy-first translation layer between civic demand and budgetary sanction.

Layer 3: Mathematical Hotspot Formulation (PHS)

Objective Multi-Criteria Decision Framework for National Policymakers

Algorithmic Formulation
The National Priority Hotspot Score Formula
PHS = 0.35 • CDI + 0.30 • IDI + 0.20 • DVI + 0.15 • FAG
Calibrated across 4 normalized sub-indices [0 to 100]

CDI (35% Weight)

Citizen Demand Index: Combines petition volume, urgency multiplier, and community Gram Sabha corroborations.

IDI (30% Weight)

Infra Deficit Index: Measurable baseline deficit in JJM piped water, PMGSY road connectivity, and health centers.

DVI (20% Weight)

Demographic Vulnerability: SC/ST tribal population %, poverty rate (BPL), and NITI Aayog aspirational rank.

FAG (15% Weight)

Fiscal Allocation Gap: Ratio of unallocated capital deficit against sanctioned Ministry project master plans.

Tier 1 Crisis: PHS ≥ 70 (Immediate Central Intervention) Tier 2 Priority: PHS 55 to 69 (Fast-Track Convergence) Tier 3 Managed: PHS < 55 (Scheduled Maintenance)
