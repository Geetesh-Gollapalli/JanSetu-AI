# JanSetu AI (जनसेतु)
Digital Public Good — Multilingual Citizen Feedback Aggregation & National Infrastructure Prioritization

## Overview
Aggregates citizen development requests across India via voice IVR, WhatsApp, Telegram, and SMS in 10+ Indic languages. 
Fuses citizen demand with Census demographics, Jal Jeevan Mission water indices, PMGSY road connectivity, and National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP) budgets to surface demand hotspots and recommend high-priority projects for PM GatiShakti and central ministries.
